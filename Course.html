ABES Engineering College offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs in various fields of engineering, technology, and computer applications. The courses are designed to provide students with strong theoretical knowledge along with practical and industry-oriented skills. Undergraduate Courses B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering – Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning B.Tech in Information Technology (IT) B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering B.Tech in Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE) B.Tech in Civil Engineering Postgraduate Courses Master of Computer Applications (MCA) Master of Technology (M.Tech) in selected engineering specializations. Course Highlights Practical laboratory training Industry-oriented curriculum Technical workshops and seminars Projects and internship opportunities Career and placement support Technical and extracurricular activities
